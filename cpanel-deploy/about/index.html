About Cortvex

Cortvex is a small, senior team building websites, apps and AI systems for companies that want to ship serious work - without the bloat of a traditional agency.

Our motto

Build Faster.
Automate Smarter.

Our mission

Help ambitious teams design, build and scale digital products that actually move the business.

Premium websites, custom apps, AI automation, chatbots, voice bots and full growth systems.

Why we are different

Small team, high ownership.
